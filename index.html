Put EK TCG on your home screen

No app store, no download fee — this page installs like an app and opens full-screen.

iPhone (Safari)
  1. Tap the Share button (square with an arrow).
  2. Tap Add to Home Screen.
  3. Tap Add — the EK TCG icon lands on the home screen.
Android (Chrome)
  1. Tap the ⋮ menu (top right).
  2. Tap Add to Home screen / Install app.
  3. Confirm — the icon lands on the home screen.
